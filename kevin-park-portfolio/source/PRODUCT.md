## Stack

Two outputs: a deployable static site with real URLs (/, /work/money-layer/, /work/money-lifecycle/) and a single-file claude.ai Artifact preview with bare-token hash routes (#home, #money-layer). Confirmed by the user: "Artifact + static bundle".

## Users

- Primary: hiring managers for Senior, Lead, and Principal Product Marketing roles in fintech, payments, financial infrastructure, developer platforms, gaming, and emerging technology. Named targets: Stripe, Coinbase, Wise. They judge whether Kevin can think strategically, work independently, understand complicated products, explain them clearly, influence product, sales, and leadership, and take a problem from ambiguity to execution.
- Secondary: recruiters (current level, expertise, progression, accomplishments, product types in about 30 seconds); executives and founders; product managers and cross-functional partners.
- English only. The Korean edition was removed at the user's request (2026-09-27).

## Product Purpose

A personal product marketing portfolio that shows how Kevin thinks, not a resume in website form. Selected case studies show the path from signals to insight, strategy, story, execution, and impact. After 30 seconds a visitor sees a senior product marketer who makes complex technology easier to understand and care about; after a full case study they know what Kevin personally did, what changed, and where the evidence ends.

## Positioning

"There's a story inside your complex product." Kevin finds the change a product makes for the people it serves and explains that value in a way people remember. Experience in fintech, payments, cross-border money movement, and gaming gives the point of view credibility.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, applications, and referrals, often skimming on a phone before reading on a laptop. The only contact route is LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/kevinswpark. No email, phone, address, or placeholder contact links, even though the resume carries them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the thinking next to the work.
2. Ownership is precise.
3. Evidence ends where it ends.
4. Finalized copy is never rewritten; design carries the energy.
5. The work leads; the biography supports.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic headings, descriptive links, WCAG AA contrast on the dark ground, alt text, reduced-motion support for every ambient and pointer-driven effect.
