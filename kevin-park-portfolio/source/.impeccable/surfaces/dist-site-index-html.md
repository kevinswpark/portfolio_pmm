## Direction contract

THESIS: Kevin's work presented the way a modern money app presents a product: one vivid card you want to pick up, figures that glow, and a calm dark ground that lets color do the selling. Refuses the category default of a light editorial portfolio with thumbnails and an about blurb.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground #08080C with raised surfaces #111117 and #191922, hairlines at 8 to 14 percent white. One brand gradient (Coinbase blue #0052FF, violet #7B3FF2, pink #FF3D8B, amber #FFB84D) used on the hero card, the story pill, the Money Lifecycle card field (violet, ending at #A8329E), the closing card, and one How I work cell. Coinbase blue #0052FF is the action color and the field of the finished Money Layer card. Signal lime #C8FF3D marks value in motion: open flows in diagrams, lit resume figures, the proof status dot, one How I work cell. Mona Sans throughout: expanded bold display (width 112 to 125), normal-width body. Big rounded cards (28px), pill controls. Stripe-style skewed gradient band with hard diagonal edges behind the hero and the article header, fading only toward the left edge.

STORY: The visitor reads the thesis line, meets the Money Layer as a card, sees two case-study cards with their tensions, reads the philosophy and experience with prior-role figures lit up, opens Money Layer, follows problem to picture to line to proof, and connects on LinkedIn.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Headline across the full width on two lines in expanded bold at clamp(2.75rem, 1rem + 5.4vw, 6rem) with "story" on a gradient pill; below it on the left, the supporting line, blue primary "Explore the work" and ghost "Connect on LinkedIn". Right: a tilted card (1.586 ratio, about 460px wide) with a slow gradient surface reading "The Money Layer for Games", linking to the case study. Skewed gradient band behind both. Floating dark glass nav with name, Work, Experience, and a blue "Connect on LinkedIn" pill. Primary action visible at 1280x720 and 390x844.

FORM: Dark neon cards (fintech app vocabulary: card, chip, pill, glowing figure), user-pinned, seed key 7e376353. Signature interaction: the hero card tilts toward the pointer with a moving sheen (lerped, hover devices only, off under reduced motion); the philosophy statement brightens word by word as it scrolls into view (starts at readable tertiary color); the band drifts slowly.
